Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough, in order. The story first, the one possibly-missable trophy guarded in Chapter 1, then collectibles, then cleanup — and the 100% Completion Gold last.

    Time and ground rules

    • Expect roughly 200–300 hours for the Platinum, per the launch guides.
    • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off on your trophy save.
    • All 55 trophies are single-player. There are no online trophies.
    • Check progress in game at any time with Touch Pad, then Progress; the 100% Completion Checklist unlocks after the story.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. The story trophies below it are story-locked — they can only be earned while their chapter or mission is live, and there is no mission replay — so a manual save per chapter is your safety net.
